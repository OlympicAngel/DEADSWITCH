# 06 — Systems: Progression, Tiers, Reboots & Long-Term Goals

## 1. Progression layers

1. **AI module tree** — restoring corrupted parts of the AI core unlocks fields and abilities (see doc 03).
2. **Base tiers** — each tier changes look, gameplay, and unlocks new systems.
3. **Cycles** — chosen relocation or forced reboot, with permanent legacy.
4. **Endless escalation** — enemies and the world scale forever, so progress is never "finished."

## 2. Base tiers

| Tier | Name (working) | Focus (draft) |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Bunker** | Survive, restore the AI, first outposts |
| 2 | **District** | Expand, defend, first faction pressure |
| 3 | **City** | Heavy faction play, cyber warfare, hidden AI agenda matures |
| 4 | **Region** | Large-scale threats, collapse events, the truth surfaces |

More tiers can be added for infinite scaling. The more you have, the more power, ability, effects, and gain you receive, and the more you must defend and sustain.

### Tier 1 pacing

**Player-paced**: the tier ends when the player is ready, or when forced by a hard event.

### Tier-up gates

To advance to the next tier, the player must clear **three gates**:

1. **Build thresholds** — reach key facility levels and a stable energy surplus.
2. **AI milestone** — restore a key module that unlocks the next tier.
3. **Human cost** — spend people to expand; the amount scales with what you've built.

(Surviving a trial event was not selected as a gate. Hard events can still force pacing.)

## 3. Visible progression

- **Visual evolution** — the base changes look with each tier, from rough bunker to fortress.
- **Battle scars** — damage, burns, and wreckage persist until repaired.
- **Corruption visuals** — the base and UI glitch and flicker as corruption rises.
- **New fields** — each module field unlock introduces a new screen, ability, or mechanic.

## 4. Reboots and relocation

> **A reboot never happens randomly.** It is either a hard penalty from player failure, or a player choice.

### Voluntary relocation (chosen reboot)

The player moves to a new location with a bonus. It gives:

- **Scaled legacy bonus** — the stronger your last base, the bigger the permanent bonus.
- **Better starting region** — richer zones or a stronger strategic position.
- **Fresh slate for the AI** — a chance to cleanse corruption and reshape its personality.

Choosing *when* to leave is a strategic decision.

### Forced reboot (hard penalty)

Can be triggered by:

- **Hub destroyed** — the main base falls while under-defended with no retreat plan.
- **Total AI takeover** — critical corruption left unresolved and the AI seizes control.
- **Population collapse** — people drop to near zero from war, famine, or sacrifice overuse.
- **Purge success** — a faction wins a full-scale purge after the player ignored repeated warnings.

### What persists across a reboot

- **Legacy modules** — some restored AI modules stay permanently unlocked.
- **Faction scars** — old grudges, alliances, and reputations carry into the new cycle.
- **Survivor legend** — a few veterans or named operators carry over with bonuses.

(Map knowledge and lore memory were not selected as carry-overs.)

## 5. Ironman mode

The AI core can be destroyed for a full wipe. A legacy bonus carries into the next run. How this differs from a forced reboot is an open task (see doc 09).

## 6. Long-term goals

- **Mastery challenges** — special goals such as surviving a purge with zero AI use, or winning with no losses.
- **Legacy score** — each base cycle earns points toward permanent perks.

(Truth endings and collections were not selected. The hidden truth remains a mystery rather than a completion goal.)
