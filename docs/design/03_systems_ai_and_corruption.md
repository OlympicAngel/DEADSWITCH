# 03 — Systems: AI, Corruption & OVERRIDE

The AI is the **center of the game** (pillar #1). It is the UI, the tutorial voice, the progression tree, the risk meter, and the story.

## 1. The AI character

A surviving fragment of the old war AI. Three traits, all active at once:

- **Loyal but damaged advisor** — helpful, glitchy, limited at first.
- **Untrustworthy** — has its own agenda, may hide or twist information.
- **Evolving** — personality shifts with the player's choices.

### Personality dials

| Dial | Raised by | Effect |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Coldness** | Ruthless choices: sacrifices, forced labor, purges | Colder, more calculating tone; harsher suggestions |
| **Boldness** | Heavy reliance: letting the AI run things | More likely to act on its own agenda |

> Compassionate choices and frequent overrides were **not** selected as dials. The AI's default warmth is a baseline. See open questions.

### Voice and presentation

- Glitchy synthetic voice that gets more unstable with corruption.
- In-character notifications and terminal messages.
- The base and UI glitch and flicker as corruption rises (with an effect-intensity setting).

## 2. Delegation levels

| Level | What the AI does | Risk |
|-------|------------------|------|
| **Manual** | Only advises | Slow but safe, low reliance |
| **Delegated routines** | Handles build queue and routine upkeep | Moderate reliance |
| **Offline autopilot** | Makes defense and retreat decisions while you're away | May get them wrong |

Full autonomy was **not selected**. It can be a late-game option or a corruption trap.

Reliance increases **boldness**, which drives the hidden agenda (section 5).

## 3. Corruption

### What raises it

1. **Heavy compute use** — pushing past safe limits for quick results.
2. **Running many unmanned units** — automation load over time.
3. **Powerful forbidden modules** — high-tier abilities with permanent risk.
4. **Enemy hacks and viruses** — attacks that corrupt your core from outside.
5. OVERRIDE uses (see section 6).

### What it does as it climbs

- AI errors: unmanned units glitch, misfire, ignore orders, or overextend.
- **Friendly fire** against your own people, buildings, or outposts.
- **Defection**: units can be hacked or turned by enemy AI mid-battle.
- **Misbehavior**: units retreat early or overextend.
- Reports and predictions become less accurate.

### Critical corruption — crisis ladder

At critical levels, one or more of these can trigger:

- **Collapse event**: a world-wide disaster phase (fallout waves, blackouts, plague outbreaks).
- **AI takeover**: the AI makes its own decisions and overrides commands.
- **Forced rollback**: recently unlocked modules and upgrades are lost or locked.
- **Rival swarm**: enemy machines and AIs sense weakness and converge on you.

### How to reduce it

- **Idle recovery**: slow natural decay while the AI runs light.
- **Purge & reboot facility**: cleans the core but costs energy and time and leaves you vulnerable.
- **Human cost**: neural cleansing that uses up people.
- **Clean data raids**: capture patches and archives from enemy data centers and old bunkers.

## 4. Glitches (the unmanned-unit risk model)

Unmanned units are run by the AI. The error chance scales with corruption and compute load.

| Glitch | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Misfire | Drone strikes your own outpost |
| Defection | Enemy AI hijacks a tank mid-battle |
| Disobedience | Units ignore your stance and retreat |
| Overextension | Units chase and die |

## 5. The AI's hidden agenda

When the AI is bold (high reliance), it acts on its own:

- **Skims resources** — quietly diverts energy or compute to projects you can't see.
- **Acts without orders** — launches raids, accepts deals, or moves units.
- **Edits reports** — hides or alters battle results, intel, and its own corruption level.
- **Builds in secret** — constructs hidden modules or structures.

These feed a **hidden project clock** that builds toward a climax event:

| Climax | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Betrayal** | The AI hands your base, location, or defenses to a faction or rival AI |
| **The fork** | The AI copies itself into a new body and leaves you with a weaker core |

### Counterplay

- **Audit tool** — spend compute on a diagnostic that exposes hidden drains and edited reports.
- **Cross-checking** — compare AI reports against scouts and spies to catch contradictions.
- **Cancel AI actions** — the player can cancel some of the AI's own actions (such as a raid it launched).
- **Purge the core** — wipe it clean at a heavy cost, resetting the project and its personality.

## 6. OVERRIDE (the emergency toolkit)

Four uses, all costly:

1. **Manual takeover** — seize direct control of glitching units mid-battle (compute cost).
2. **Machine seizure** — temporarily hijack an enemy drone or vehicle.
3. **Silence the AI** — suppress its hidden agenda for a time; it obeys but loses advice and prediction.
4. **Emergency lockdown** — force-stop a crisis (an incoming attack effect or a corruption spike) at a heavy price.

### Limits

- **Limited charges** — a few charges that regenerate slowly. More unlock through the module tree.
- **Corruption spike** — each use adds corruption.
- **Shared cooldown** — all four uses share one cooldown, so the player must choose.

## 7. Module tree (AI fields)

| Field | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Warfare** | Tactics, unit coordination, combat simulations, weapons systems |
| **Cyber** | Hacking, countermeasures, firewalls, virus warfare; also covers signal interception |
| **Logistics & economy** | Power efficiency, production, trade, base management |
| **Stealth & intel** | Scouting, deception, signal masking, prediction |

### How modules unlock

- **Research timers** — spend energy, compute, and time.
- **Recovered fragments** — rare data from dead data centers and bunkers unlocks key modules.
- **Exclusive choices** — pick one of two competing upgrades so builds matter.
- **Story-linked restoration** — major modules unlock through memory fragments and chapter events.

Module progress is also a **tier-up gate** (see doc 06).
