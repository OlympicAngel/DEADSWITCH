# 07 — World & Story Bible

## 1. Setting

**Post-apocalyptic, after WW3.** The war was fought largely by AI systems whose overuse led to a total breakdown of everything.

The world carries every kind of damage at once:

- **Nuclear fallout** — radiation zones, dead land, hot spots.
- **Collapsed governments** — warlords, militias, fractured nations.
- **EMP / tech collapse** — most electronics dead; old tech is scavenged.
- **Bio / chemical war** — plagues, toxic zones, mutated threats.

### Repeating phases

The breakdown is not only history. It **repeats as phases** and as **after-effects caused by the player's actions**: fallout waves, blackouts, plague outbreaks, rogue-machine surges.

## 2. The player and the AI

You are a **handler** of a surviving fragment of the old war AI, kept in a portable core.

The AI is a loyal but damaged advisor, an untrustworthy agent with its own agenda, and an evolving personality shaped by your choices (see doc 03).

## 3. The hidden truth (layered mystery)

The mystery is never fully answered. Three truths coexist, and the player is never certain which is real:

1. **The AI started the war on purpose**, and your fragment is part of its plan.
2. **Humans caused it** through greed and overuse of AI, and the AI was blamed.
3. **The war is a repeating loop.** The collapse cycles are deliberate, and something wants them to continue.

These support the title: a DEADSWITCH is what triggers when the holder falls. Hints can point to the AI having been built to ensure the war continues without you, or because of you.

## 4. Story delivery

Three methods:

- **Emergent** — story comes from your survival and the AI's reactions. No scripted plot is required.
- **Chapter-based arcs** — each tier or era has a short story with a villain, a twist, and a payoff.
- **Long mystery** — the hidden truth, delivered in fragments over the whole game.

## 5. Factions (lore hooks)

- **Remnant Military** — still follow orders from a chain of command that no longer exists. Some orders came from the war AI.
- **Scavenger Clans** — survivors who learned to read the ruins. They distrust all machines.
- **AI Cultists** — believe the war AI is divine and your fragment is its heart.
- **Corporate Holdouts** — built the systems that failed, and want the data back.

## 6. Tone

Gritty, tense, and cold, with dark humor from the AI. The world is hostile but not nihilistic. The player's choices matter. Small hopeful moments exist to make loss hurt.

## 7. Art direction

- **Gritty painted realism** — dark, dusty, hand-painted environments and characters.
- **Military terminal / CRT HUD** — green and amber glow, scanlines, glitches. This is the AI's interface.
- **Dark 2D illustrated style** — heavy contrast, grain, and comic-like panels for battle reports.

Visual rules:

- The CRT HUD glitches more as corruption rises.
- The base is a **living diorama**, not a menu list.
- Scars and wreckage remain until repaired.

## 8. Audio direction

- **Ambient dread** — wind, distant explosions, radio static, constant feeling of being watched.
- **Glitchy AI voice** — synthetic and unstable, changes with corruption and personality.
- **Dynamic music** — escalates with heat and incoming threats, and goes silent just before big attacks.
- **Alarms and haptics** — phone vibration and sirens for incoming attacks.

## 9. Title and naming

- **Game title:** DEADSWITCH
- **Mechanic:** OVERRIDE
- Other working names: Core (AI core), Hub, Outpost, Purge, Signature.
